# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + Motion. Marketing landing page with a contact CTA; RSC for the shell, client islands only for motion.

## Users

Founders and operators of private companies who want to know who little fella is and how to send a deal. Secondary: anyone who lands on the company mark and needs a clear next step.

## Product Purpose

A public company landing page for little fella, a small personal-run fund that invests in private companies. Success is: a visitor understands the vehicle in one viewport, believes the thesis, sees real companies, and can send a note.

## Positioning

A personal fund, not an institution. Operator-led, founder-friendly, structure-agnostic. Hunts underpriced private deals in hard sectors (energy, atoms, machines) and holds without a forced exit clock. The name is the position: little fella, not a marble-and-serif fund.

## Operating Context

Visited on a laptop or phone. One page. Primary action is contact. No login, no portal, no LP raise flow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single marketing page with a working contact CTA.
- Contact email is undecided; the page must keep the address in one place so it can be swapped.
- No fund raise, no AUM, no invented LPs or testimonials.
- Marks / multiples (2.26x, 1.53x, 1.00x) stay off the public page unless later approved.
- Domain is undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Public name: **little fella** (always lowercase). Formal: little fella ventures / LFV.
- Wordmark: heavy geometric sans, lowercase, tight tracking. Assets in `public/brand/` from `/Users/anthonybassi/Downloads/little fella`.
- Brand blue: `#4193FF` (sampled from the wordmark field). Off-white type on blue. Alternate mark: off-white wordmark on charcoal with corner brackets.
- Voice: direct, operator, no fund-brochure swagger. No "we partner with exceptional founders."
- Visual references the owner bound: Prime Intellect, Zero University, Awwwards / landing.love craft, Blunarova editorial restraint. Not Sequoia / a16z.
