# Design

Built world for little fella, 2026-08-16.

## Surface

Single marketing page. Persuade. First viewport is the wordmark on brand blue. Reading happens on charcoal. Close returns to blue with a note form.

## Tokens

- Blue `#4193FF` (sampled from the wordmark field)
- Cream `#F3F1EC`
- Charcoal `#1C1C1C`
- Mute `#C9C4BB`
- Type: Satoshi, self-hosted. Black for display, medium for body. Lowercase for the brand.
- Radius: 0
- CTA: underline text, or cream fill on blue for the form submit

## Do not

Inter, purple mesh, three feature cards, glass, eyebrows, section numbers, performance multiples, invented AUM, em dashes.
